## Executive summary

**APPROVED:** School years are never permanently deleted. Non-current years are intended to leave the normal active list through archival, while their records remain recoverable for correction. Exactly one year stays current; changing it requires a 10–1000-character reason and before/after audit. A year and its term dates lock once any billing account exists for that year. Financial and audit records are preserved. Grade levels are fixed (Kinder and Grades 1–12), and `/settings/school` shows one grade at a time.

**PROPOSED:** Give school years a distinct persisted archive timestamp; keep their existing terms and exam periods attached and historically readable, without cascading term state. Keep independent term archiving out of the first implementation unless explicitly approved. Give sections a distinct archive timestamp instead of treating every existing `active=false` section as archived. Preserve identifiers and references on archive/restore. These choices need the owner decisions below.

## ADM-07 — term lifecycle (Phase 5C scope)

| Option | Advantages | Risks / compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Recommended: derive visibility from parent year, no independent term archive** | No term migration or cascade; terms/exam periods remain attached and visible under an archived year and reappear when the year is restored. Preserves FKs, sequence, dates, and audit records. | Does not supply a separate “Remove term” action; owner must accept deferral. Parent-archived write rules still need approval. |
| B — Independent `terms.archived_at` and archive/restore endpoints | Supports correcting an accidentally configured term without hiding its year; can retain exam periods by reference. | Requires migration, filtered API, separate action/audit events, conflict handling for term sequence and exam-period visibility; restore must not silently reactivate a parent-archived year. More states to test. |

**PROPOSED for A:** Archiving a year changes only the year state. Terms and exam periods are historically visible beneath it, keep the same IDs/sequence/date fields, and are never automatically archived or restored. While the parent is archived, do not create terms/exams or edit their dates; whether audited name-only corrections are allowed is an owner decision. On restoration, existing term/exam rows naturally appear again. If B is chosen later, keep term sequence reserved across archived and active rows, retain exam periods, and ensure term restore under an archived parent cannot make it operationally active. The current rule does not require term or exam dates to lie within the year; that unrelated open policy is not decided here.

## ADM-07/ADM-08 — section lifecycle (original audit mapping)

| Option | Advantages | Risks / compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Map Archive to `active=false` | No migration; current student options already hide inactive sections. | Existing inactive sections would be retroactively labeled Archived with no archive actor/time, and ordinary PATCH can “restore” them. It cannot distinguish temporary inactivity from archival. |
| **B — Recommended: separate `sections.archived_at`, retain `active`** | Archive and inactive status remain distinct; existing inactive rows are not reclassified. Restore preserves the section's previous active flag and identity. Archive event has durable provenance. | Additive migration and changes to options/direct-write validation are needed; every section selection path must exclude archived rows. |

**PROPOSED for B:** Settings Active view shows unarchived active and inactive sections with truthful badges; Archived view shows archived sections only. Archive/restore set or clear `archived_at` on the same section ID, never delete or null existing `students.section_id`. Current student list/profile keeps the referenced section name with an Archived indicator where context allows; do not relabel a student's enrollment automatically. New student assignments and changes to a different archived section must fail server-side; an update that retains the student's existing archived section ID may remain valid so unrelated corrections are possible. Section names remain reserved within a grade across both views, using the current uniqueness rule; restoration therefore cannot create a duplicate unless an unsafe future uniqueness change is introduced. Allow rename of an archived section only if the owner approves it; use existing case-insensitive name validation, same ID, and a distinct audited correction event or a clear `section.updated` before/after record. Archive/restore require Administrator `settings.system.manage`, separate `section.archived`/`section.restored` audit events, same-transaction state and audit writes, and conflict responses for already archived/restored rows. No grade-level rename or archive is proposed.

**Minimum API if B is approved:** Extend `GET /api/settings/grade-levels` with explicit section archive state for authorized settings readers, or add a scoped section list that filters by grade and archive view when server pagination is needed. Add `POST /api/settings/sections/{section}/archive` and `/restore`; preserve the existing PATCH for ordinary name/availability edits while preventing it from clearing archive state. Review `GET /api/student-record-options` and direct `POST/PATCH /api/students` together, since the options endpoint alone is not a write guard. If independent term Option B is approved, add `POST /api/settings/terms/{term}/archive` and `/restore`, explicit term archive state in year reads, and server checks for parent and exam-period writes. These are proposed contracts only.

## Data-preservation and acceptance criteria proposed for implementation

1. An Administrator can archive only a non-current, unarchived year; a current year remains unchanged on rejection. Existing reasoned year switch remains separate; exactly one current year stays current. Accounting cannot archive/restore.
2. Archived year and its terms, exam periods, billing accounts, installments, payments, receipts, fee schedules, audit history and report values retain their original IDs and values. The settings Active view omits it; Archived view and authorized historical account/report paths can still read it.
3. Restoring the same year ID changes only archive state; it does not make it current, duplicate children, or lift date locks. Label corrections remain audited; uniqueness/overlap checks include archived years.
4. Under term Option A, year archive/restore does not alter term/exam rows. Term/exam history is visible under the archived year; any disallowed write returns a clear validation/conflict response.
5. Under section Option B, archive/restore retains the same section ID and all student links. Previously inactive sections remain inactive, not automatically Archived. Archived sections are unavailable for new assignment through both options and direct POST/PATCH, while authorized users can read existing assignments.
6. Names stay unique per grade across both section views; attempted duplicate creation/rename/restore fails without partial writes. Both archive and restore record actor, before/after, and time atomically. Audit failure rolls back the transition.
7. School Settings retains one-grade-at-a-time navigation, selected grade across view changes, keyboard-visible actions, confirmations, accessible statuses, responsive overflow and pagination. API tests use fictional linked records; browser acceptance checks both Administrator and Accounting views.

## Decisions requiring owner confirmation

1. **Year with financial references:** May a non-current year with accounts/payments be archived while all existing authorized historical payment/correction paths continue under their present rules? Recommended **yes**, with no financial mutation.
2. **Archived-year corrections:** Is label-only correction while archived sufficient, with restoration required for other unlocked edits? Recommended **yes**; locked dates never unlock.
3. **Year historical visibility:** Should archived years stay selectable in authorized historical account/report filters while leaving the normal settings Active list and new-creation choices? Recommended **yes**. The current-only student statement remains current-only.
4. **Term scope:** Choose A (recommended: terms/exams follow parent visibility only, no independent term Archive/Restore) or B (independent term lifecycle). If A, confirm whether name-only corrections under an archived parent are allowed; recommended **restore parent first**.
5. **Section state:** Choose A (reuse inactive) or B (recommended: distinct archive timestamp and keep `active` separate). If B, confirm existing inactive sections remain in Active view; recommended **yes**.
6. **Archived section assignments and edits:** Confirm existing student links remain visible, new assignments are rejected, and retaining the same archived section on an unrelated student edit is allowed. Recommended **yes** to all three. Confirm whether archived sections may be renamed; recommended **yes, audited and uniqueness-checked**, for correction.
7. **Restoration and naming:** Confirm restore returns a year as non-current and a section to its prior `active` value, with names reserved across archive states. Recommended **yes**; no automatic cascade or name reuse.

No recommendation above is an approved implementation policy. Once these decisions are answered, implement in separate backend/database and frontend phases, with migration approval and isolated fictional-data regression tests before any live deployment. The earlier Phase 5B browser checks and unrelated audit blockers remain open.
