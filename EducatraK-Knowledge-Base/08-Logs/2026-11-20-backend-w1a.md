## Rules enforced
- Start date must be before end date. School years cannot overlap.
- Unique values: labels, grade names and section names within a grade (case-insensitive); term sequence within a year; grade sort order.
- School-year and term dates lock once any billing account exists for that year. Labels and names stay editable and audited.
- make-current needs a 10–1000 character reason. It locks every school-year row and switches in one transaction. The audit entry records the before and after current year and the reason.
- The first school year ever created becomes current.
- Each mutation and its audit entry commit or roll back together. This is tested with an injected audit failure.
