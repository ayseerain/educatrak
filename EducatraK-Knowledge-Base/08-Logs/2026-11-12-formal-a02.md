| Payment reconciliation and allocation | Locally verified | `RecordPaymentAction` locks the account and installments in a transaction, rejects overpayment, allocates oldest due item first, preserves a payment balance-after snapshot, and recalculates statuses. | Idempotency and receipts | Locally verified | `PaymentController` binds a request checksum to the user/key and persists/replays its response in the payment transaction. Matching-MySQL duplicate-key and lock behavior is not verified. |
| Void compensation | Locally verified | Void request/decision actions lock the payment, request, account, and installments; approval restores paid/balance values and recalculates status. Focused coverage exercises separation of duties and balance restoration. The existing B09 audit-atomicity issue still applies because controller audit writes occur after action transactions. |
| Billing adjustment safeguards | Code-reviewed | `ApplyBillingAdjustmentAction` locks the account, rejects zero amounts and disallowed reductions, and prevents total due below paid or a negative balance. No dedicated focused adjustment test was added in this read-only audit. |

This can alter existing student balances contrary to the approved requirement. |

| Immutable financial/audit history | Partial | Versioned fee rows, receipts, payment snapshots, and void records exist. The existing B09 issue remains: several controllers commit a business mutation before separately writing its audit entry, so an audit-insert failure can leave a mutation without required audit history. |
