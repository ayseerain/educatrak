## Confirmed gaps

1. `PaymentController::store` persists `payment_confirmation` rows with `queued` status but neither dispatches a delivery job nor invokes a provider. The payment's reported email-queued state is therefore log-only.
2. `ReminderDeliveryController::send` and `::retry` call email/Twilio delivery inside the request. This is synchronous, rather than queued/after-commit delivery, and ties request timing to provider availability.
3. A successfully accepted reminder email remains `queued`; the repository has no approved email delivery-confirmation mechanism that could transition it to `sent`.

## Remediation update — Thursday, November 12, 2026 — 17:08:29 Asia/Manila

- **Delivered fix:** Added `DeliverNotification`, a one-attempt queued job. Payment confirmation and reminder delivery now dispatch it after persistence; payment creation and notification retry use after-commit dispatch. The job retains `queued` after provider acceptance, records failures, and preserves the existing reminder-enabled fail-closed check.
- **Retry:** Cashier may retry a failed payment-confirmation email or an eligible failed reminder. The notification row is locked in its retry transaction so a subsequent retry cannot enqueue another delivery.
- **Validation:** Focused B07/payment coverage passed (27 tests / 313 assertions); the full isolated SQLite PHPUnit suite passed (149 tests / 1,058 assertions); Pint and `git diff --check` passed.
- **Remaining gates:** No email delivery-confirmation policy/provider, sender/daily-send-limit policy, deployment queue worker, or target-provider sandbox proof is available. No real message was sent.
