# Phase 5C — Archive and Restore Policy Reconciliation

> **Phase 5C-B superseding owner decision, November 26, 2026:** The owner subsequently directed that archiving a school year never changes historical collection totals or hides its payments from historical report detail. The older report-scope exclusion and Archived-report requirement below are retained as superseded decision history only. Active/Archived scope applies to School Settings management views. The owner also clarified that active and inactive sections are archive-eligible, restoration preserves `active`, and a section with assigned students must be reassigned before archive. The backend implementation and current contract are recorded in the Phase 5C-B addendum to the formal Per-Role Audit Report and the backend archive contract handoff.

**Status:** Planning only. This report records the owner's seven responses in this continuation and separates settled policy from implementation design. It does not authorize application changes, migrations, database writes, or deployment. **ADM-05, ADM-07, and ADM-08 remain open** until implementation and tests prove the approved behavior.

## 1. Executive Summary

The owner approved non-destructive archive and same-record restoration for non-current school years, preservation of linked financial data, label-only correction while archived, parent-attached terms, a distinct section archive state, retention of student-section links, and restoration without duplicate IDs or name reuse. Archived-year records **and their payments** must leave ordinary report rows and collection totals until restoration. Authorized Accounting users must be able to inspect them through an explicit **Archived** scope in existing account/report pages. Existing account-read role access must also remain intact; this does not grant Cashier a report capability. This is a scope change to reported figures, not a change to stored transactions; reports and exports must label their scope truthfully. No totals or financial records were changed during this planning phase.

The minimum compatible design appears to need additive archive state for school years and sections, server-side transition guards and audit events, settings Active/Archived views, and coordinated report-query rules. Terms and exam periods remain attached to their existing school year and need no independent archive column or endpoint. This is a technical proposal, not approval to migrate or implement.

## 4. School-Year Archive and Restore Proposal — ADM-05

**Existing:** No persisted archive field or endpoints. `is_current` identifies the current year. Year labels are unique; date ranges cannot overlap any configured year. Accounts lock year and term dates. Ordinary account creation is current-year-only. Settings GET includes all years.

**APPROVED lifecycle:** Archive only a non-current year; leave its ID, label, dates, terms, exams, fee schedules, billing accounts and financial/audit history intact. Move it from the ordinary School Settings list to an Archived view. Permit audited label correction while archived. Restore the *same* year as non-current. A separate, reasoned `make-current` action remains necessary if the restored year should become current. The existing account-based year/term date lock survives archive and restore. No automatic child archive/restore occurs.

**PROPOSED minimum technical contract:** Add nullable indexed `school_years.archived_at`; do not replace `is_current` or use deletion/soft-deletion scopes. Reuse `GET /api/settings/school-years` with an explicit Active/Archived view filter and archive state in its response. Add Administrator-only `POST /api/settings/school-years/{schoolYear}/archive` and `/restore`, with row lock, atomic `school_year.archived`/`school_year.restored` audit record, and clear 409 for invalid transitions. `makeCurrent` must reject an archived target and archival must reject the current target. Keep uniqueness and overlap checks across active **and** archived years. No `archived_by` column is necessary if the audit log remains the actor source; adding one would require a separate demonstrated need. All endpoints/fields here are proposals until reviewed.

**Approved report scope:** Ordinary report rows and collection totals exclude payments/accounts linked to archived years. An explicit Archived scope in existing account/report pages permits authorized Accounting review of those retained records. Existing `accounts.view` access for Cashier/Accounting must remain intact on account pages; `reports.view` does not expand to Cashier. Archive and restore may therefore change what an ordinary dated report shows, without changing any stored payment. Proposed API scope fields, labels, totals, and export parity require focused implementation review and regression tests before release.

## 5. Term and Exam-Period Lifecycle Proposal — ADM-05 dependency / Phase 5C ADM-07 wording

**APPROVED:** Terms and exam periods stay attached to their archived year, retaining IDs, order, dates, FK relations and audit history. They are visible within the archived year's historical settings detail and hidden from ordinary active-year management by the parent view filter. Restoring the parent restores access to the existing children without touching child rows. There are no independent term Archive/Restore controls and no cascading state write. While the parent is archived, its terms/exams are read-only under the approved “restore before other edits” rule; existing account-based date locks still apply after restoration.

**Implementation consequence:** No term or exam migration is needed. Add server guards to term/exam create/update routes when their parent is archived, including direct API calls. The current sequence uniqueness `(school_year_id, sequence)` remains across all terms. The existing “term/exam dates within school-year bounds” question is separate and remains unchanged. Any future independent term archive request would require a new owner decision and separate schema/API review.

## 6. Section Archive and Restore Proposal — original ADM-07/ADM-08

**Existing:** `active=false` hides a section from student option lists but the settings API still returns it. Ordinary PATCH can reactivate it; no archive actor/time or dedicated Archived view exists. Direct student writes can still reference an inactive section if its ID and grade match.

**APPROVED distinction and effects:** Archival is separate from `active`. Existing inactive rows remain inactive in the Active view. Archived sections move into a dedicated Archived view for the selected grade. Preserve each section's ID, grade and `active` value; on restore, clear archive state so the original active/inactive value resumes. Preserve all `students.section_id` references; do not transfer students. Existing student display retains its section name and should identify archived status where the API supplies it. Reject a new section assignment to an archived ID through both picker and direct POST/PATCH, while allowing an unrelated edit that retains the student's already assigned archived section. Reserve section names across both views; archived rename is allowed only with current uniqueness checks and an audit entry. The grade itself cannot be renamed or archived.

**PROPOSED minimum technical contract:** Add nullable indexed `sections.archived_at`, leaving `active` unchanged. Extend authorized grade/section read responses with archive state and a view filter or add a scoped paginated section read if needed. Add `POST /api/settings/sections/{section}/archive` and `/restore`, protected by `settings.system.manage`, with row lock, atomic distinct audit actions, and 409 for invalid transitions. Keep `PATCH /api/settings/sections/{section}` for ordinary name/availability updates, but prevent it from bypassing archive state. Update `GET /api/student-record-options` and direct student-write validation together; provide enough read-only section state in student responses if an Archived indicator is shown. No new role capability, student FK rewrite or name-uniqueness relaxation is proposed.

## 8. Database Integrity and Migration Considerations

1. Use additive nullable columns on the existing `school_years` and `sections` tables if the proposed timestamp representation is accepted; existing rows remain unarchived. Review target MySQL indexes/collation and backfill behavior in a disposable matching instance before deployment.
2. Keep FK references untouched. A year with billing or fee schedules, and a section with students, is archived by state, never deleted, reassigned or cloned. Do not use a global Eloquent scope that accidentally hides historical relations or misstates report totals.
3. Preserve existing label/date overlap and case-insensitive section name validation across archive states. Retain the database unique grade/name key. Lock rows during transitions and write audit event in the same transaction; audit failure must roll back state.
4. The current database permits zero or multiple `is_current` rows despite application safeguards. Archive implementation must preserve application invariants and must not treat this phase as permission to redesign current-year selection.
5. A pre-existing inactive section remains inactive, with no invented archive timestamp or actor. Restore of a newly archived section returns its stored `active` value. Do not import the staff-account anonymization timer or modify Ledger Import rules.

## 9. Role and Permission Considerations

Administrator retains the existing `settings.system.manage` write capability; Accounting retains `settings.system.view` read access. Super Admin, Cashier, Auditor and Registrar gain no School Settings archive control. Student assignment protection belongs on the server even when the picker hides archived choices. Archived account reads follow existing `accounts.view` (Cashier/Accounting); Archived reports follow existing `reports.view` (Accounting). The current-only student statement remains current-only unless separately specified. This proposal does not grant Administrator financial visibility. Every successful transition and correction must include actor, time, entity ID and before/after values using the existing audit infrastructure. Disabled or hidden frontend controls do not replace a server permission check.

## 10. Risks and Compatibility Requirements

- **Financial accuracy:** The owner approved excluding archived-year payments from ordinary daily totals, so a previously issued dated report can show a different total after archival. This is a query-scope change, not a financial-row mutation. Both ordinary and Archived views/exports must show their scope clearly, and deterministic tests must prove that their constituent records remain intact and restore reverses the scope change.
- **Direct-write bypass:** Student option filtering alone does not prevent assignment to an archived section. The backend must reject a changed/new archived section ID while permitting approved unchanged historical linkage.
- **Student history limit:** The schema holds one `students.section_id`, not a dated assignment history. Archive/restore preserves the association currently stored and existing audit events; it does not invent earlier section assignments. Adding a longitudinal section-history feature would require a separate requirement and design.
- **Stale clients and concurrent actions:** Existing clients expect `SchoolYear`/`Section` response fields; add fields compatibly. Invalid current/archive/restore transitions and duplicate writes need clear conflict responses. Current-year switch and archive actions must serialize correctly.
- **Visibility and history:** Archived years/sections remain reachable in settings Archived views. Accounting inspects retained financial records through an explicit Archived scope in existing account/report pages. Avoid model-level default scopes that accidentally hide account/payment relations from authorized archive reads; do not invent a new screen.
- **ID mapping:** Keep ADM-07's original section-action issue title in the 33-item audit. Term lifecycle is an ADM-05 dependency discussed by later prompts under ADM-07 wording.
