# Login page audit

Audited: Monday, November 02, 2026 — 14:14:31 +08:00

Scope: Current `frontend/src/features/auth/LoginPage.tsx`, assessed against the requested two-column school-login composition and the EducatraK design system. No product code was changed in this audit.

1. **HIGH — The required school photograph is absent from the widget** (R-37, R-40). The left panel renders green background, diagonal decorative bands, brand copy, and a seal instead of `loginpage_bg.jpg`. This conflicts with the requested photograph-only left section and gives the screen a decorative treatment unrelated to its purpose. Replace the panel contents with the supplied photograph as a high-priority `<img>` using `object-cover`, reserving its rendered space.

2. **MEDIUM — The form’s visual hierarchy is weaker than the requested unified login widget** (R-37). The photo-led, balanced 50/50 composition is missing, while the form is anchored in a narrower right column (`1.1fr 0.9fr`) and its desktop branding is separated from the form in the decorative left panel. Use a balanced two-column grid, place EducaTrak branding above the form on the right, and keep the photograph purely institutional context.

3. **LOW — Several page-local treatments drift from the established component system** (R-10, R-11, R-12). The login widget uses a raw border, a bespoke large shadow, a translucent/blurry background, and diagonal color bands. They add visual noise without assisting sign-in. Use the design-system white surface, `rounded-lg`, and `shadow-card`/`shadow-raised`; remove decorative overlays and blur.

4. **LOW — The password visibility control does not follow the shared interactive-state pattern** (R-39). It uses a custom circular treatment and disables with `pointer-events-none`, which conflicts with the project’s shared guidance for disabled controls. Keep the existing behavior and accessible label, but use the standard 44px control, shared focus treatment, and `cursor-not-allowed` disabled state.
